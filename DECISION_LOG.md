## DEC-001 — Reject Declared Dependency as Primary Data Thesis

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** Closed / Rejected  
**Stage:** Pre-repository research

### Decision
Do not build Monopolises primarily as a database of dependencies declared in SaaS 10-K Item 1 / Item 1A filings.

### Basis
A closed 40-filing feasibility test reported:

- useful dependency disclosures existed, but were frequently unnamed;
- the named layer was weak;
- year-to-year state deltas were sparse;
- inference burden was high.

### Boundary
The raw evidence from that pre-repository test is not yet imported into this repository. These reported results are retained as decision provenance, not as a public statistical claim.

## DEC-004 — Close MON-G0-RH and Reject Strategic Replaceability v0.1 as the Primary Measurement Thesis

**Date:** 2026-08-28
**Status:** Closed / Binding
**Gate:** MON-G0-RH
**Supersedes:** DEC-002 as the active category hypothesis

### Decision

Close Gate `MON-G0-RH` as **FAIL**.

Do not scale **Replacement Horizon** into the primary measurement architecture of Monopolises.

Do not treat **Replacement Asymmetry** or **Option Compression** as ratified category primitives on the basis of Gate 0.

Strategic Replaceability v0.1 is rejected as the primary build thesis **in its tested measurable form**.

### Basis

The fixed five-case set was completed without rescue cases.

- 5/5 produced useful evidence-bounded research records.
- 0/5 produced a single canonical strategic Replacement Horizon without an unresolved methodological choice.

Recurring blockers included start-event ambiguity, open endpoints, non-source-native sufficiency thresholds, capability/capacity separation, moving technological frontiers, qualification constraints, and supply-chain boundaries.

### Boundary

This decision does not assert that strategic replacement difficulty is economically irrelevant.

It asserts that the tested public-evidence model did **not** establish Replacement Horizon as a sufficiently reproducible core metric to justify category-scale construction.

The five case records remain valid research artifacts. Their recurring distinctions remain findings only and are not automatically promoted into a successor thesis.

### Construction consequence

Until a new thesis is separately authorized:

- no global replacement graph;
- no Replacement Horizon score;
- no Replacement Asymmetry score;
- no Option Compression score;
- no entity ranking;
- no scaled programmatic entity or sector pages;
- no ratified standard based on the failed metric;
- no monetization product based on the failed metric.

### Next-thesis rule

Any successor thesis must begin with a new decision and a new falsifiable gate.

MON-G0-RH is not reopened, broadened, or retroactively redefined to produce a pass.

## DEC-005 — Ratify Layered Monopolisation as the Monopolises v0.2 thesis

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Closed / Binding
**Gate:** MON-G1-LI — Closed / PASS
**Supersedes:** DEC-004 as the active thesis posture (DEC-004's closure of MON-G0-RH stands unchanged)

### Decision

Ratify **Layered Monopolisation** as the Monopolises v0.2 thesis: control concentrates **layer by layer**, recorded as `System × Layer × Control Mechanism × Evidence`, classifiable from public primary evidence without inferring legal monopoly status.

Gate `MON-G1-LI` is closed **PASS** on the performance of the fixed eight-case set (`research/v0.2/GATE_MON-G1-LI_EVALUATION.md`): all four pass conditions hold; no falsifier triggers.

### Active taxonomy (exactly four evidenced layers)

- `legal_exclusivity` — evidenced by MON-G1-S8
- `capacity_control` — evidenced by MON-G1-S1 (single-actor) and MON-G1-S2 (collective)
- `access_gatekeeping` — evidenced by MON-G1-S6, MON-G1-S4, MON-G1-S5
- `switching_dependency` — the held-out probe, triggered by a named source-native instrument in MON-G1-S7, is admitted

### Research candidates (outside the active taxonomy — not placeholders)

- `qualification_control` — not evidenced (the S6 legal candidate was `access_gatekeeping`; "qualified/sufficient" language did not establish it)
- `standard_interface_control` — not evidenced (no primary source showed a named interface *required to operate* the frozen function; a rulebook (S5) and a named-but-not-required interface (S3/CUDA) both failed)
- `temporal_constraint` — never targeted by a case; untested, not refuted

Non-proof is information, not a reserved slot. A candidate enters the active taxonomy only when a case evidences it under a gate.

### Construction consequence

This PASS authorizes, **in order**, and only from this decision onward: a layer ontology, an interface thesis, and a data architecture — each built on the four active layers only. It does **not** authorize scores, rankings, entity/sector pages, a ratified standard, or monetization.

### Discipline for the successor build

- Every published record carries `System × Layer × Control Mechanism × Evidence` with a **dated, scope-bounded** claim boundary, **including jurisdiction where jurisdiction is load-bearing**.
- No market-share/dominance figure **alone**, and no antitrust/competition conclusion, may carry a classification (S2 stays excluded).
- Dated findings are records of their date; later change does not erase them, and present-tense restatement requires current primary evidence.

### Next-gate rule

Any scored, ranked, or scaled surface — or admission of a new layer — requires a **new decision and a new falsifiable gate**. MON-G1-LI is not reopened, broadened, or retroactively redefined.
