## DEC-001 — Reject Declared Dependency as Primary Data Thesis

**Date:** 2026-08-27  
**Status:** Closed / Rejected  
**Stage:** Pre-repository research

### Decision
Do not build Monopolises primarily as a database of dependencies declared in SaaS 10-K Item 1 / Item 1A filings.

### Basis
A closed 40-filing feasibility test reported:

- useful dependency disclosures existed, but were frequently unnamed;
- the named layer was weak;
- year-to-year state deltas were sparse;
- inference burden was high.

### Boundary
The raw evidence from that pre-repository test is not yet imported into this repository. These reported results are retained as decision provenance, not as a public statistical claim.

**Status:** Active
