Monopolises / Thesis

Adopted thesis · DEC-005 · 29 August 2026

Control concentrates layer by layer.

The unit is System × Layer × Control Mechanism × Evidence. The work classifies supported mechanisms within dated and bounded systems—not entire companies as monopolies.

Four evidenced layers

What the sources established

Each type was admitted only after appearing in the fixed eight-case evidence set.

Legal exclusivity

Exclusive rights or restrictions created by a defined legal instrument. Example: U.S. letters over post routes, with exceptions.

Case S8

Capacity control

A documented productive-capacity locus that the bounded function cannot readily bypass. Example: EUV scanners in the 2021 supply-chain review.

Cases S1, S2, S6

Access gatekeeping

A documented rule governing admission to a specified platform, network, or procurement channel.

Cases S4, S5, S6

Switching dependency

A source-native instrument tied to moving away from a provider or service, without assuming every customer is locked in.

Case S7

Essential limits

Findings have boundaries.

  • A control layer is not a legal monopoly finding or an accusation.
  • Market share or market dominance alone does not assign a layer.
  • Every positive layer needs a mechanism, a precise locus, primary evidence, and an explicit excluded-claims boundary.
  • Negative and ambiguous outcomes are valid; the method must sometimes refuse a classification.
  • No company rankings, monopoly scores, probabilities, or global coverage claims are authorized.

Why v0.1 was rejected.
