Strategic replaceability

This page preserves the first repository-era thesis that Monopolises tested. It is no longer an active category hypothesis.

Proposition tested

Power is the time it takes to replace you.

The candidate measurement was Replacement Horizon.

Gate result

The fixed test did not produce a canonical strategic interval without unresolved choices about start, sufficiency, qualification, frontier, or supply-chain boundary.

Boundary

The failure does not establish that replacement difficulty is irrelevant. It establishes that the tested public-evidence model was not reproducible enough for category-scale construction.
