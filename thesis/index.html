Working thesis · v0.1

Strategic replaceability

Market share alone does not describe every form of strategic power. Monopolises is testing whether the time required to replace a critical function can reveal a different and useful structure of dependence.

Core proposition

Power is the time it takes to replace you.

This is a research proposition, not a universal law and not a legal classification. It becomes useful only if the underlying replacement interval can be evidenced reproducibly.

What the project is testing

Replacement Horizon is the candidate term for the evidenced interval required for a viable replacement to become available for a defined function under stated conditions.

Replacement Asymmetry describes a potential condition in which two sides face materially different evidenced replacement horizons.

Option Compression is a candidate descriptive term for a reduction in viable alternatives inside a specified decision horizon.

Boundary

Monopolises does not infer legal monopoly status, wrongdoing, or market dominance from replacement difficulty. It will not invent precision where the sources provide only ranges, milestones, or uncertainty.
