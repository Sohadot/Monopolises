# Interface Thesis — Monopolises

**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Archived with MON-G0-RH — not binding for any successor thesis

> This interface thesis was written for the Strategic Replaceability v0.1 measurement thesis, which was rejected when Gate MON-G0-RH closed as FAIL (see `DECISION_LOG.md` DEC-004 and `research/GATE_0_CLOSEOUT.md`). It is retained as a research record. It is **not** binding for the public foundation and does not constrain any successor thesis, which must begin with its own decision and its own falsifiable gate.

## Core rule

> The interface must not decorate the asset. It must embody the asset's thesis.

Monopolises tested time-to-replace as a lens on strategic power. The interface was therefore meant to make **time, alternatives, constraint, and evidence** legible without pretending that an unverified metric was already known.

## Visual grammar

- **Open field:** viable optionality.
- **Bounded interval:** replacement horizon under evidence.
- **Interrupted path:** a constraint or missing evidence.
- **Multiple endpoints:** alternative routes.
- **Source marker:** a claim boundary tied to evidence.

## Prohibited during Gate 0

- monopoly meters;
- entity power scores;
- animated world maps implying measured global coverage;
- decorative 3D chokepoints;
- red danger styling that implies wrongdoing;
- fake live data;
- visual precision exceeding evidentiary precision.

## Performance and access

The foundation must remain static-first, keyboard-readable, screen-reader compatible, responsive, and useful without animation or JavaScript.
