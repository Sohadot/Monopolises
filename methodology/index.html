Research methods · v0.2

Every classification must survive its evidence.

The method separates what an instrument says, what follows mechanically, and what would require an analyst to supply missing meaning.

Evidence discipline

Three classes. Different authority.

S0 · Source-native

What the source states

Statutory language, operator rules, documented constraints, or other facts stated in an admitted primary instrument.

S1 · Reproducible derivation

What follows without discretion

A transparent inference from S0 facts under a frozen rule, with derivation shown explicitly.

S2 · Interpretive synthesis

What must not carry the classification

Broader legal, economic, or viability judgments may inform a discussion but cannot establish a layer on their own.

Falsifiable sequence

Tests before adoption.

All recorded dispositions are specific to the frozen v0.2 test set and the contracts tested. They are not production-grade system certification.

Integrity by design

The method may refuse.

Each record preserves a dated scope, instrument, locus, evidence class, and what the sources do not justify. The CUDA case records no_evidenced_control_layer under the admitted evidence. A separate structural fixture tests the ambiguous_layer representation.

Versions, fixtures, source citations, negative tests, and historical gate failures are retained for review.
