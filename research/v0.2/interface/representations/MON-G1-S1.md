=== CLAIM_EVIDENCE_ROW ===
CLAIM: Sole production of EUV scanners essential for ≤5 nm patterning
EVIDENCE_CLASS: S0
SOURCE: White House / Dept. of Commerce, 100-Day Reviews under EO 14017, June 2021, p. 51
FACT: for lithography, ASML (Netherlands) is the sole producer of EUV stepper/scanners, which are essential for producing integrated circuits with a linewidth of 5 nm or less.

=== CLAIM_EVIDENCE_ROW ===
CLAIM: Only ASML supplies EUV equipment (contrasted with broader lithography oligopoly)
EVIDENCE_CLASS: S0
SOURCE: White House / Dept. of Commerce, 100-Day Reviews under EO 14017, June 2021, p. 54
FACT: only ASML supplies EUV equipment, and the top three providers (ASML, Nikon, Canon) account for virtually all of the overall market share.

=== CLAIM_EVIDENCE_ROW ===
CLAIM: EUV light-source capacity is vertically integrated into the scanner producer
EVIDENCE_CLASS: S0
SOURCE: White House / Dept. of Commerce, 100-Day Reviews under EO 14017, June 2021, p. 50
FACT: Cymer produces the lasers (light source) for ASML's EUV stepper/scanner machines; ASML acquired Cymer in 2013; Cymer remains a separate operating unit within ASML.
