Can replacement horizons survive evidence discipline?

The current gate tests whether a reviewer can reconstruct a bounded replacement result from cited source-native facts and mechanical derivations, without hidden assumptions.

Current output restriction

Before Gate 0 closes, this site will not publish a global replacement graph, scores, rankings, or scaled entity pages.

What counts as success

A qualifying case must be reconstructable from the cited evidence. If the conclusion depends materially on an analyst deciding that an alternative is “good enough,” the case does not establish the metric.

What counts as failure

If useful cases repeatedly collapse into interpretive judgment, the category thesis is rejected or narrowed rather than rescued with more complexity.
