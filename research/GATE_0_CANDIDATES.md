# Gate 0 Candidate Set — Monopolises

**Gate:** MON-G0-RH  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** FIXED BEFORE EVIDENCE EXTRACTION  
**Freeze date:** 2026-08-27  

## Purpose

This file freezes the Gate 0 candidate set before formal evidence extraction.

The set is intentionally heterogeneous. It is not a market map, an ontology, or a claim that all five cases will produce a measurable Replacement Horizon. A `not_evidenced` result is valid.

Reconnaissance was used only to confirm that primary-source pathways exist. No candidate may be replaced because its evidence later proves inconvenient.

---

## MON-G0-C01 — RD-180 → domestically powered NSSL launch capability

**Strategic system:** National-security space launch  
**Critical function:** Certified U.S. medium/heavy national-security launch capability without reliance on the Russian-manufactured RD-180 engine.  
**Current source/system:** Atlas V / RD-180 dependency in the U.S. national-security launch enterprise.  
**Replacement target:** A domestically powered launch system certified for National Security Space Launch missions.  
**Why selected:** The endpoint is unusually discrete: certification is an official qualification event, and the case contains a long documented transition with delays.  
**Primary-source pathway:** U.S. GAO; U.S. Space Force / Space Systems Command.  
**Known evidentiary challenge:** The start boundary may have multiple legitimate anchors (statutory direction, funded development, vehicle-development start). Gate 0 must not silently choose the anchor that produces the cleanest horizon.

---

## MON-G0-C02 — Domestic HALEU availability

**Strategic system:** Advanced nuclear fuel  
**Critical function:** Domestic availability of high-assay low-enriched uranium for U.S. civilian advanced-reactor deployment.  
**Current source/system:** DOE-documented absence / insufficiency of domestic commercial HALEU supply during the program baseline.  
**Replacement target:** A defined domestic HALEU supply state that can be shown from primary evidence to satisfy a stated requirement.  
**Why selected:** DOE publishes explicit production quantities, program dates, allocations, and enrichment/deconversion actions.  
**Primary-source pathway:** U.S. Department of Energy / Office of Nuclear Energy / NNSA.  
**Known evidentiary challenge:** Production milestones are not automatically equivalent to a sufficient replacement supply. If demand sufficiency cannot be established without S2 judgment, the result must be `not_evidenced`.

## MON-G0-C03 — U.S. rare-earth magnet manufacturing capacity

**Strategic system:** NdFeB rare-earth magnets  
**Critical function:** Domestic production of strategically important rare-earth permanent magnets.  
**Current source/system:** Publicly documented U.S. reliance on foreign sources for rare-earth magnets and related processing capacity.  
**Replacement target:** A domestic production state whose capacity and timing are explicitly documented and whose sufficiency can be established without hidden assumptions.  
**Why selected:** The 10X project provides explicit commissioning timing and a stated total U.S. production-capacity milestone.  
**Primary-source pathway:** U.S. Department of Defense; MP Materials statutory/company disclosures where needed.  
**Known evidentiary challenge:** A 10,000-metric-ton capacity milestone does not by itself prove full replacement of foreign supply. Sufficiency must be sourced, not assumed.

---

## MON-G0-C04 — U.S. leading-edge semiconductor fabrication

**Strategic system:** Leading-edge logic semiconductors  
**Critical function:** Domestic high-volume manufacturing of leading-edge logic process technology.  
**Current source/system:** U.S. dependence on overseas leading-edge fabrication during the CHIPS-program baseline.  
**Replacement target:** Domestic high-volume production at a clearly specified leading-edge process node / technology level.  
**Why selected:** TSMC Arizona has explicit fab milestones, process-node commitments, and production schedules in government and company primary sources.  
**Primary-source pathway:** U.S. Department of Commerce / CHIPS Program; TSMC official disclosures and annual reports.  
**Known evidentiary challenge:** The technological frontier moves while replacement capacity is being built. A node that is leading-edge at project start may not be leading-edge at completion. Gate 0 must distinguish capability arrival from complete strategic replacement.

---

## MON-G0-C05 — U.S. natural-graphite active-anode-material capacity

**Strategic system:** Lithium-ion battery active anode material (AAM)  
**Critical function:** Domestic large-scale production of natural-graphite AAM.  
**Current source/system:** DOE-documented import dependence and China-centered high-purity graphite/AAM supply.  
**Replacement target:** Domestic AAM production capacity sufficient for a defined U.S. requirement, if that requirement and the relevant capacity are both source-native.  
**Why selected:** DOE sources expose project capacity, production milestones, and a quantified U.S. demand context, allowing the Gate to test whether partial capacity can be distinguished from replacement.  
**Primary-source pathway:** U.S. Department of Energy / Loan Programs Office / manufacturing program records.  
**Known evidentiary challenge:** A domestic facility can reduce dependence without replacing the incumbent supply base. The existence of new capacity must not be mislabeled as replacement.

## Freeze rules

1. These five candidates are fixed for MON-G0-RH v0.1.
2. No candidate may be removed because the evidence yields `not_evidenced`.
3. No sixth case may be added to rescue a weak result.
4. Formal source admission begins only when evidence extraction starts for a case.
5. S0 and reproducible S1 may establish a bounded result.
6. S2 may explain uncertainty but may not establish the Gate 0 Replacement Horizon.
7. The Gate closes on the performance of this fixed set, not on selected successful examples.
