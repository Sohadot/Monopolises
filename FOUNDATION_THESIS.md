## Gate outcome

Strategic Replaceability v0.1 was tested through Gate `MON-G0-RH`.

The fixed five-case test did not establish a single canonical strategic Replacement Horizon in any case without an unresolved methodological choice about start, endpoint, sufficiency, qualification, frontier, or supply-chain boundary.

Accordingly, this thesis is **not ratified as the primary scalable measurement thesis for Monopolises**.

The text below is retained as the historical proposition that was tested.

---

## Proposition tested

> **Power is the time it takes to replace you.**

The candidate term **Replacement Horizon** meant the evidenced time interval required for a viable replacement to become available for a defined function, under stated conditions.

**Replacement Asymmetry** and **Option Compression** were candidate research terms only.

## Boundary

Monopolises must not:

- label an entity a monopoly merely because replacement is difficult;
- infer legal wrongdoing from structural dependence;
- fabricate replacement time;
- turn analyst opinion into source-native fact;
- publish pseudo-precision.

## Gate finding

Evidence-bounded transition records can be constructed from public primary evidence.

A single canonical strategic Replacement Horizon was not reproducible enough across the fixed set to support category-scale construction.

See `research/GATE_0_CLOSEOUT.md` and DEC-004.
