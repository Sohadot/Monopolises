Monopolises / About

Independent inspectability · Governed by evidence

Make claims easier to challenge.

Monopolises is a Sohadot research asset: a public method for examining precisely scoped control mechanisms and refusing claims that primary evidence cannot support.

Who can use the research

Designed for critical readers.

Researchers, journalists, policy and competition analysts, technical specialists, and strategic decision-makers can use the published case records as inspectable starting points—not as legal findings, current-market ratings, or investment recommendations.

Each research claim leads back to its system scope, dated source, control instrument, locus, and excluded alternatives. A negative finding remains visible where evidence does not support classification.

Operating discipline

The record survives disagreement.

Evidence before assertion

Source-native facts and reproducible derivations carry classifications; broad interpretation does not.

Rejection stays visible

The failed v0.1 measurement line is preserved as provenance, alongside v0.2 negative and marginal results.

Governance before scale

Append-only decisions constrain what is adopted, published, or later operationalized.

Current boundary

A ratified research model, not yet a product.

The Layered Monopolisation v0.2 thesis, ontology, interface thesis, and logical data architecture are adopted. Production databases, APIs, scored products, ranking surfaces, and monetization remain unauthorized by DEC-008. This site reports the approved research state; it does not claim an operating observatory.
