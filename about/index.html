About the asset

Build the reference before claiming the category.

Monopolises is a governed digital asset in research. Its public surface is intentionally smaller than its ambition.

Operating principles

Evidence precedes scale. Public claims remain traceable. Synthetic pages are prohibited. GitHub is the source of truth. The interface must embody the thesis without overstating what has been measured.

Current boundary

Monopolises is not an antitrust rating service, monopoly accusation database, or investment recommendation product. Strategic replaceability remains a hypothesis under validation.
