Strategic replaceability · research hypothesis

Power is the time it takes to replace you.

Monopolises is testing whether economic and geopolitical power can be described through a simple but demanding question: when a critical source, system, or capability disappears, how long before a viable replacement actually arrives?

Alternatives that arrive too late are not operational alternatives.

Interface thesis

Make the replacement window visible.

The visual system uses time, constraint, and alternative routes as its grammar. It does not display monopoly scores or invented certainty.

Candidate language

Replacement Horizon

The evidenced time interval required for a viable replacement to become available for a defined function, under stated conditions.

Replacement Asymmetry

When two sides face materially different evidenced replacement horizons with respect to each other.

Option Compression

A research term for a reduction in the set of viable alternatives within a specified decision horizon.

Evidence before category

The thesis is allowed to fail.

Gate 0 asks whether replacement horizons can be reconstructed from public primary evidence without hidden analyst assumptions.

Until that gate closes, Monopolises will not publish a global replacement graph, entity power rankings, monopoly scores, or scaled SEO pages.
