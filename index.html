Where does control concentrate?

Complex systems can contain distinct points of control. Monopolises identifies where control sits, how it operates, and which primary evidence supports each bounded finding.

Not a list of monopolies. A method for examining control, layer by layer.

The reading instrument

Four questions behind every positive finding.

  1. SystemWhich precise function and dated scope are being examined?
  2. LayerWhich kind of control is evidenced at that locus?
  3. MechanismWhat rule, capacity, or switching instrument creates the constraint?
  4. EvidenceWhich primary source supports the claim, and what does it exclude?

System × Layer × Control Mechanism × Evidence. Each record is dated and scope-bounded.

Claim discipline

Structural analysis is not an accusation.

These records do not establish antitrust wrongdoing, monopoly power, or a universal ranking. Dated evidence may support a specific control instrument without supporting a claim about a whole industry.

The earlier Strategic Replaceability v0.1 measurement thesis failed its gate. Its research remains visible as an honest predecessor, not as the current method.
