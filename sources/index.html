Read with care

Historical records are not live market feeds.

The v0.2 case evidence was gathered in August 2026 and describes specific dated conditions, instruments, and jurisdictions. A later change in law, business practice, or market structure requires new primary evidence before a present-tense restatement.

Some sources support a refusal rather than a positive classification. For example, NVIDIA's FY2025 10-K supports a named ecosystem description but not, by itself, a source-native switching barrier.
